A growing picture of the code

Scroll down the commit history, and the dots will appear in time order. Scroll up, and you can go back to an earlier time. You can also drag the time slider directly to see it.

Chart controls and data notes

By moving the mouse over the points, you can view the details. Drag a box starting in the blank area in the figure to select multiple commits. The time is displayed according to the time zone when the commit was recorded and will not change depending on the viewer's time zone. The filter's cutoff is shown in UTC.

The data here is based on the saved committed code snapshot, and only counts the lines of code that still exist in that version. The area of the circle represents the number of lines left by each commit, not the total number of lines changed at that time.

Keyboard: Tab to a dot for details, Enter to reach its commit link, and Escape to close the details. Use the arrow keys on the time slider. Download the line data.
