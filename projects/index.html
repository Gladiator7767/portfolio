Campus Mobility Dashboard

Fictional concept · Design exercise

This concept explores how a dashboard could help students compare ways to travel around a fictional campus.

  • Sketch a map of walking, cycling, and shuttle routes.
  • Explore filters for transport mode and departure time using simulated trips.
  • Compare chart designs for communicating travel time and uncertainty.

Reading Habits Explorer

Fictional concept · Design exercise

This concept considers how an interactive reading journal could make patterns in reading time and book choices easier to explore.

  • Design a small simulated reading log with dates, genres, and reading duration.
  • Experiment with a weekly timeline and genre filters.
  • Compare ways to present missing entries without treating them as zero reading time.
